## Highlights

**Visualize goes 3D and full screen.** `@Visualize` apps can now draw shaded 3D scenes natively, and any app can expand to fill the app on desktop and Android. Desktop also gains a rendering diagnostics overlay and uses less CPU while idle, scrolling and animating.

### Native 3D scenes

- New `g.scene3d({camera, light, viewport}, d => …)` draws boxes, spheres, cylinders, cones, tori and custom meshes (`ui.box`, `ui.sphere`, `ui.cylinder`, `ui.cone`, `ui.torus`, `ui.mesh`) plus 3D polylines, each with its own position, rotation, scale and colour.
- Scenes are projected, depth-sorted and flat-shaded natively, then painted as batched GPU paths. Solid primitives have their back faces culled; custom meshes are double-sided. Painter's ordering means intersecting geometry can sort imperfectly.
- Bounded like the 2D API: at most 4 scenes per canvas, 64 objects and 8,192 triangles per scene, and 2,048 vertices / 4,096 faces per custom mesh. Invalid cameras or geometry fail explicitly.
- The prompt contract is now version 3 with a 3D section. Replies made with the previous contract are still recognised. A new **solids** sample shows orbiting, drag-to-rotate and a speed slider.

### Full-screen Visualize

- A new expand button in each Visualize card's header opens the app full screen. On desktop it fills the window; on Android it gets its own screen with a back button. Esc, the button again, or Android back returns to the thread.
- Drawings scale to fill the width (or about 80% of the height for tall canvases) without asking for a particular size. Drawings are now clipped to their viewBox, so they no longer spill past the canvas when scaled.

### Desktop

- **Rendering stats** (Settings → Developer, or ctrl-shift-r) show frame pacing, late frames, draws per second, process CPU, transcript rebuilds and Visualize layout/paint cost.
- Lower CPU: the activity spinner is capped at 30 fps and the transcript does less work per frame. Measured on a 120 Hz display, idle CPU while a session is working dropped from about 14% to 5%, and scrolling from about 21% to 16%.
- View Source in Visualize cards scrolls on its own instead of scrolling the chat.
- The composer grows with multi-line drafts instead of covering its attachment, mention and model controls.

### Android

- Expanding **Thinking** now shows the model's reasoning. It appears before the reply, says "Thinking" while it streams, and leaves out redacted reasoning.

### Compatibility

Existing Visualize apps, saved state and historical prompts keep working. No app-data or credential migration is required. Stock Pi and Durable remain pinned to **1.2.0**.

### Validation

Formatting, strict Clippy and workspace tests (including new 3D projection, full-screen and composer tests and the Android UI tests) were run locally. Release CI builds against the pinned, patched Zed and packages the supported desktop targets and the Android APK.
